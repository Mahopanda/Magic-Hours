Magic Hours does not collect any of your personal data. Your photos, edits, and the filters you make stay on your device.


### Your photos are processed on your device

Live preview, applying styles, exporting, learning a look from a photo, and importing LUTs all run on your device. Magic Hours has no servers. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send your photos or any other data to us or anyone else.

Photos you take or edit are saved in the app's own folder (the original, the result, and the edit settings) so you can re-edit them later. Location data is removed from the original before it is saved there. Filters you make are stored in the app as well. The "learn a look from a photo" feature only analyzes color statistics from the reference photo; the photo itself is not kept.

Deleting a photo from the library, a filter, or the app removes that data. If you use iCloud Backup or computer backups, this data is included in your own device backup, which is handled by Apple under its policies. We cannot access it.

### No accounts, no tracking

No sign-in or account is required. The app contains no ads, analytics, crash-reporting tools, or third-party SDKs. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other apps or websites.

### Permissions

- **Camera**: used only for live preview and taking photos in the in-app camera. The camera feed is processed on your device. The app takes still photos only; it does not record video or audio.
- **Add to Photos**: used when you save finished photos to your photo library. The app asks for add-only access and never asks to read your library.
- **Picking photos and files**: the app uses the system photo picker and file picker, so it only receives the items you choose.
- **Location**: Magic Hours never asks for location access. Photos you export, share, or save to Photos contain **no location data**. Capture time and camera information are kept.
- **Motion**: the camera screen reads the accelerometer to tell whether the phone is upright or sideways. This is used only to rotate icons and is not stored.

### Preferences

The app remembers a few settings on your device, such as favorite and recently used styles, the last export format, and whether to auto-save to Photos. These stay on your device.

### Sharing

Photos and filter files leave the app only when you tap Save to Photos or Share. Once shared to another app, that app's privacy policy applies. Shared filter files (.mhfilter, .cube) contain only color data, the filter name, and adjustment settings. They do not contain photos.

### Children

Magic Hours does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

### Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the date above. Any change that involves collecting data will be clearly disclosed in the app.
